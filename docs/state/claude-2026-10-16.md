### What happened, in order

1. **"Put the tools below the banner, horizontal scroll; banner autoswipe and
   manual swipe; slides for samoshop and ประกาศ."** Loaded the apple-design
   skill. Chose native scroll-snap over a hand-animated track (1:1 touch and
   platform momentum for free); JS adds autoplay, mouse drag with momentum
   projection, holds (press/hover/visible focus/hidden/offscreen/pause button),
   reduced motion = no autoplay. The pause button is there because WCAG 2.2.2
   requires one for content that moves on its own for >5 s. The shop slide uses
   the storefront's palette by adding `.hero-slide--shop` to the token selector
   in `shop-storefront.css` (one home). The ประกาศ slide names the pinned post
   else the newest, and shows its cover only if it has a real one — the stock
   Unsplash placeholder would read as news. The news slide is a dark island OFF
   the `--ink` scale on purpose (that scale flips in dark mode).
   The rail renders from the registry through the launcher's own renderer; it
   replaced the bottom "เริ่มต้นกับ SAMO" strip.
2. **Two carousel drag bugs, both 1-in-5 flakes in the driver**, both found by
   instrumenting events rather than rerunning: (a) a drag whose moves were
   coalesced away read as a click and OPENED the slide link; (b) a short quick
   flick (120 px at 816 px/s, measured on production) projected to 1.54 slides
   and rounded back. Write-ups in `docs/mistakes/frontend-ui.md`.
3. **"Should ข้อมูลของฉัน move to a separate page?"** Yes — Apple's account-row
